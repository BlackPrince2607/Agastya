Delete your Agastya account

Google Play account deletion request · Last updated August 1, 2026

You can delete your Agastya account and associated cloud data in either of these ways:

  • In the app (fastest): Profile → Delete account (you must be signed in).
  • By web request: submit the form below. We will verify ownership of the email and delete your account, palm images, sessions, and reading history within 14 days (usually sooner).
Deletion is permanent. Premium unlocks tied to the deleted account cannot be restored to that account.

Request deletion by email

Tapping submit opens your email app with a prefilled message to privacy@sharvo.online. Include the email you used to sign in to Agastya and the phrase Please delete my Agastya account.

What we delete

  • Supabase auth user
  • Session and reading data
  • Palm capture files in private storage
