Common topics

  • Premium not unlocking after payment: Open the app → Profile → Check premium status. If you paid with Razorpay, reopen the return link or wait a few minutes for confirmation.
  • Palm scan issues: Use good lighting, show a flat open palm, and grant camera permission.
  • Delete account: In-app Profile → Delete account, or web deletion form.
